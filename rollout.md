## Incremental Rollout Plan

### Phase 1 – Foundation
- Define LMS requirements and scope
- Pilot a small set of general knowledge modules
- No changes to existing in-world training

### Phase 2 – Integration
- Tie LMS completion to selected in-world exercises
- Introduce soft gating for qualifications or billets
- Begin converting lecture-heavy sessions into scenarios

### Phase 3 – Expansion
- Introduce rating-specific A School tracks
- Expand LMS content only as needed
- Provide guidance for instructors

### Phase 4 – Steady State
- LMS handles baseline knowledge consistently
- In-world training remains short and social
- Periodic review and adjustment based on feedback
