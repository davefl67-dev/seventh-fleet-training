SEVENTH FLEET TRAINING – QUICK OVERVIEW

Purpose:
This framework updates Seventh Fleet training for Second Life roleplay while keeping standards clear, consistent, and manageable.

Core Idea:
Separate book learning from in-world roleplay execution.

Foundational knowledge is completed off-world in an LMS. In-world time is used for short, scenario-based sessions focused on interaction and decisions.

Why This Is Needed:
Members have limited time, and long lecture sessions reduce participation. At the same time, no structure creates uneven performance. This model keeps training useful without turning roleplay into a classroom.

How It Works:
1. LMS (off-world):
- Short self-paced modules (about 5-10 minutes)
- Covers terminology, rank structure, rating fundamentals, and RP expectations
- Open-book, low-stress quizzes with retakes
- Used to unlock specific drills or qualifications
- Does not block general roleplay or social participation

2. In-World Training:
- 20-45 minute sessions
- Scenario-driven and story-focused
- Minimal lecturing
- Focus on applying knowledge through interaction and choices
- Built to be repeatable and optional

A Schools:
Rating A Schools are kept simple and RP-appropriate: a small set of LMS modules plus a limited number of short practical scenarios. Goal is confidence and consistency, not a long pipeline.

Key Principles:
- Protect roleplay time
- Keep training short and usable
- Use soft gating for specific activities
- Support instructors by reducing burnout
- Keep training fun, practical, and sustainable

Outcome:
Faster onboarding, better baseline knowledge, stronger in-world sessions, and better long-term retention across the fleet.

This is not about lowering standards. It is about putting the right training in the right place.
