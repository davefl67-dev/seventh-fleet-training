## Anticipated Concerns and Responses

### “Isn’t this making training too casual?”
No. Standards still exist where they matter. This framework preserves structure and expectations while removing unnecessary friction that does not translate well to Second Life.

### “Will members actually complete LMS coursework?”
Short, self-paced modules are easier to complete than long in-world lectures. LMS completion unlocks participation in activities members want to attend, providing a natural incentive without punishment.

### “Does this reduce realism?”
It reduces burden, not realism. Terminology, structure, and scenario design remain authentic, while timelines and workloads are compressed to suit a roleplay environment.

### “Is this replacing in-world training?”
No. It protects in-world training by removing book learning that disrupts pacing. In-world sessions become more engaging and interactive.

### “Is this adding administrative overhead?”
The LMS reduces repetitive instruction and instructor burnout by standardizing baseline knowledge. Administrative effort shifts from repetition to facilitation.
