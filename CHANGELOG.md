## v0.1 — Initial Framework  
**Date:** 2026-02-08

### Added
- Initial proposal for a split training model separating LMS coursework from in-world roleplay
- Definition and purpose of an off-world Learning Management System (LMS)
- Emphasis on short, scenario-driven in-world training sessions
- Soft gating philosophy that unlocks activities without blocking social roleplay
- Concept for simplified, RP-appropriate rating “A Schools”
- Incremental rollout approach to allow gradual adoption

### Notes
- This version establishes intent and direction
- It is explicitly proposed and evolving
- No final software platforms or enforcement mechanisms are defined
