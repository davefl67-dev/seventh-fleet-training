# Contributing Guidelines – Seventh Fleet Training Framework

This repository contains a proposed and evolving training framework for a Second Life roleplay group. Contributions are welcome, but must align with the core intent of the project.

## Guiding Principles

- Roleplay comes first
- Training must remain fun, accessible, and sustainable
- This framework supports realism of tone, not real-world workload
- Second Life time is leisure time, not obligation

## What This Repository Is For

- Improving clarity and readability
- Refining roleplay-friendly training concepts
- Adjusting structure based on leadership guidance or member feedback
- Correcting errors or ambiguity

## What This Repository Is Not For

- Simulating real-world Navy training hour-for-hour
- Adding mandatory time commitments
- Introducing academic rigor or gatekeeping
- Expanding scope without leadership discussion
- Turning guidance into hard policy

## Source of Truth

- README.md is the authoritative reference
- quick-review.txt is a derived artifact for Second Life distribution

## Tone and Style

- Plain language
- Professional but approachable
- No jargon for its own sake
- No punitive or disciplinary framing

When in doubt, err on the side of simplicity and fun.

## Working with AI Tools (Codex, Copilot, etc.)

This repository supports AI-assisted editing, particularly through tools such as Codex in VS Code.

When using AI tools:
- Treat the AI as a drafting or editing assistant, not a policy authority
- Keep changes incremental and reviewable
- Prefer improving clarity over expanding scope
- Do not allow AI tools to introduce enforcement language, requirements, or workload

AI tools operating in this repository are guided by `AGENTS.md`.  
If behavior or output feels misaligned with the project’s intent, adjust prompts or intervene manually.
